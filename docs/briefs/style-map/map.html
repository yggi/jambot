Style: a map of music

Mock for the Style sheet. A preset is a place; the band's style is a point that moves; entropy is how far it may wander, drawn as its reach, not as a fifth slider. Map axes: across, harmony (a static vamp → moving, rich); up, drive (legato and sparse → staccato and dense, the mean of articulation and energy, which correlate 0.84 over the presets). The third, groove (sync), is the strip below. Faint dots: the library's fragments (where the composer finds parts).

    How to write it
      
    
        harm Roman numerals in the bar's key: i7 VI III V7/iv bVII ii°. A progression follows along, so bass, pads and lead re-voice themselves.
    
        bass, lead intervals from the chord root: 1 3 5 7 9 are the chord's own tones, 2 4 6 the scale tones between, b7 or #4 alter them. Each goes to the nearest octave; ^ forces up, v down, within the part's range (change it with range=55-79). Absolute notes like a4 work too.
    
        pad, stab ch plays the current chord, nx the next one.
    
        drums bd sd gh rim hh oh rd ph.
    
        Rhythm a line fills one bar: a b sequence, [a b] squeeze into one slot, a@3 longer, a*4 repeat, a(3,8) Euclid, <a b> one per bar, a? maybe, , layers, ~ rest. legato=.5 shortens notes, :1…:9 sets a velocity.
    
        sound lines set the built-in synth, e.g. sound lead tri=0 sq=1 lpf=900.
